# Management of Target Spot in Soybean (Corynespora cassiicola)

## Symptoms and Identification
Target Spot is caused by *Corynespora cassiicola*:
- **Target rings:** Large, reddish-brown circular to irregular necrotic lesions (up to 10-15 mm) exhibiting characteristic concentric rings resembling an archery target board.
- **Chlorotic Halos:** The dark necrotic center is surrounded by a prominent yellowish (chlorotic) border.
- **Tearing & Perforation:** Dead brittle centers crack and fall out, giving leaves a tattered, torn appearance.
- **Premature Defoliation:** Lower canopy leaves drop prematurely during rainy, humid weather (>85% RH and 25-32°C).

## Immediate Actions
1. **Fungicide Application:** Spray Fluxapyroxad + Pyraclostrobin (Priaxor) @ 120 ml/acre OR Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
2. **Water Drainage:** Improve drainage in furrows; stagnant moisture accelerates disease progression.
3. **Bio-agent:** Spray *Trichoderma viride* @ 5 g/L if disease incidence is at initial onset.

## Preventive Measures
- Practice 2-year crop rotation with non-host crops (corn, sorghum).
- Use certified treated seeds with Thiram + Carbendazim (2:1 @ 3g/kg seed).
