# Management of Frogeye Leaf Spot in Soybean (Cercospora sojina)

## Symptoms and Identification
Frogeye Leaf Spot is a major foliar fungal disease of soybean caused by *Cercospora sojina*.
- **Lesion appearance:** Small, circular to angular spots (1-5 mm diameter) with light gray to tan or bleached centers bordered by prominent dark reddish-brown to purple margins.
- **Shot-hole effect:** As lesions age, the thin papery centers may drop out, giving the leaf a perforated or "shot-hole" appearance.
- **Progression:** Initially appears on upper foliage during pod filling and can spread rapidly under warm (25-30°C), humid (>80% RH) conditions.

## Immediate Control Actions
1. **Fungicide Spray:** Apply Pyraclostrobin 20% WG (@ 200 g/ha or 1 g/L) OR Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC (@ 1 ml/L).
2. **Timing:** Spray at the initiation of flowering (R1-R3 stage) or at the first appearance of leaf spots.
3. **Bio-control:** Foliar spray of *Trichoderma harzianum* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (@ 5 g/L) in early stages.

## Preventive Management
- Plant certified resistant cultivars recommended by ICAR-IISR (e.g., JS 20-34, NRC 127).
- Rotate crops with non-host graminaceous crops like maize, sorghum, or pearl millet.
- Deep summer ploughing to bury infected crop residues.
