# Management of Septoria Brown Spot in Soybean (Septoria glycines)

## Symptoms and Identification
Septoria Brown Spot (*Septoria glycines*):
- **Speckled lesions:** Small, irregular dark brown to black spots (1-4 mm) developing initially on primary and lower unifoliate and trifoliate leaves.
- **Chlorosis:** Spots coalesce into irregular necrotic brown patches surrounded by extensive bright yellow (chlorotic) tissue.
- **Upward spread:** Infection moves upwards from lower canopy towards upper leaves during extended periods of warm rain and wet weather.

## Immediate Treatment
1. **Protective Fungicides:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L OR Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
2. **Systemic Combinations:** Spray Tebuconazole 10% + Sulphur 65% WG @ 2.5 g/L OR Azoxystrobin + Tebuconazole.
3. **Foliar Nutrition:** Spray 1% Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) to enhance leaf vigor and disease tolerance.

## Preventive Management
- Avoid continuous soybean monoculture; rotate with cereal crops.
- Seed treatment with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS @ 2.5 g/kg seed.
