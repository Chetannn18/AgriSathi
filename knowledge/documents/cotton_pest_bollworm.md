# Integrated Pest Management for Cotton Bollworm and Sucking Pests

## Symptoms & Infestation Indicators
- **Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*):** Rosetted flowers that fail to open fully, bore holes plugged with excreta on green bolls, premature boll opening with stained lint and destroyed seeds.
- **Sucking Pests (Whitefly / Jassids):** Upward curling and yellowing of leaf margins ("hopperburn"), sticky honeydew secretions with sooty mold fungal growth on foliage.

## Immediate Field Actions
1. **Pheromone Traps:** Install 8–10 pheromone traps per acre with Gossyplure septa for monitoring moth activity. If moth catch exceeds 8 per trap for 3 consecutive days, economic threshold level (ETL) is reached.
2. **Yellow & Blue Sticky Traps:** Erect 15–20 yellow sticky traps per acre at crop canopy height to capture adult whiteflies and thrips.
3. **Destruction of Dropped Bolls:** Collect and destroy shed squares and rosetted flowers daily to break the larval life cycle.

## Bio-rational and Recommended Interventions
- Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma bactrae* @ 60,000/acre at weekly intervals starting 45 days after sowing.
- Neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%) or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm (5 ml/L) for initial sucking pest repression.
- For ETL breach of Pink Bollworm: Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (0.3 ml/L) or Spinosad 45% SC (0.3 ml/L) during evening hours.
