# Weather Context and High-Humidity Disease Risk Management

## Environmental Risk Triggers
- **Continuous Relative Humidity > 80%:** Enhances spore germination of Phytophthora, Alternaria, and Colletotrichum within 6 to 12 hours of leaf wetness.
- **Intermittent Rains + Temperatures 22°C–30°C:** Ideal breeding window for fungal leaf spots, downy mildew, and bacterial leaf streaks.
- **Waterlogging in Furrows:** Deprives roots of oxygen, triggering damping-off (*Pythium spp.*) and root rot complexes.

## Field Advisory Actions
1. **Ensure Field Drainage:** Open peripheral trenches and unblock irrigation drainage channels so standing water recedes within 2 hours of rainfall.
2. **Postpone Chemical Sprays:** Never apply foliar chemicals immediately preceding rainfall. Ensure a minimum rain-free window of 3–4 hours or incorporate a silicone-based wetting sticker agent.
3. **Canopy Aeration:** Remove suckers and lower senescent foliage to allow wind penetration and faster drying of leaves.
4. **Soil Drenching:** In nursery and early transplant fields, drench root zones with *Trichoderma asperellum* (10 g/L) or Copper Oxychloride (3 g/L) to prevent collar rot.
