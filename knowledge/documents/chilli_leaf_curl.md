# Chilli Leaf Curl Virus and Thrips/Mites Complex Management

## Symptoms & Identification
- **Leaf Curl Virus (Begomovirus):** Upward curling, severe puckering and crinkling of leaves, reduction of leaf blade size, shortened internodes resulting in stunted bushy plants. Transmitted primarily by whiteflies (*Bemisia tabaci*).
- **Thrips Infestation (*Scirtothrips dorsalis*):** Upward curling of leaves into boat-shaped margins, silvering or bronzing on leaf undersides.
- **Mites Infestation (*Polyphagotarsonemus latus*):** Downward curling ("inverted boat" shape) of leaf margins, thick leathery brittle leaves, petiole elongation ("rat tail" appearance).

## Immediate Practical Actions
1. **Rogueing:** Uproot and burn severely infected viral stunted plants in the early vegetative stage to prevent vector whiteflies from acquiring and spreading the virus.
2. **Barrier Crops:** Grow 2-3 rows of tall barrier crops (maize, sorghum, or pearl millet) along field borders to physically intercept windborne whitefly vectors.
3. **Moisture Maintenance:** Avoid water stress, which drastically escalates thrips populations in dry weather.

## Integrated Advisory
- Install silver or yellow reflective plastic mulches to repel vector insects.
- Spray Neem oil 10,000 ppm (2-3 ml/L) or *Lecanicillium lecanii* entomopathogenic fungus (5 g/L).
- For severe vector pressure: Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP (1.2 g/L) or Spiromesifen 22.9% SC (1 ml/L) for mite control.
